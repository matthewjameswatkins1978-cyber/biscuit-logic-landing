Example property: Seabird Cottage (fictional)

Review scope: homepage, accommodation page, availability, rates and contact page.

Purpose: help a first-time visitor understand how to make a direct enquiry.

Priority findings

Priority 1 · Proposed fix

Make the date-checking route visible near the top

Illustrative issue: booking enquiries might rely on a contact link tucked away in the footer. Suggested action: add one prominent “Check dates and enquire” button in the first screen, linking to the existing availability or contact route.

Priority 2 · Proposed fix

Explain what happens after an enquiry

Suggested action: say whether the owner replies with a quote, whether a deposit is needed, and when a guest should expect a response. Confirm all factual details with the owner before publishing.

Priority 3 · Proposed fix

Keep price and stay conditions in one place

Suggested action: make any minimum stay, cleaning charges and key extras understandable before the visitor sends an enquiry. Avoid quoting a total that depends on dates unless it is accurate.
